# Bone Room

## Problem
Mystery fans, friends, and nerdy, curious people want a quick mystery they can actually
solve themselves, without a long game or a download.

## Initial requirements
- R1: The game shows the details and facts of a case, one clue at a time.
- R2: The player can guess who the killer is from a list of suspects.
- R3: The player wins by naming the killer in three tries or fewer, and loses otherwise.

## Round 2 requirements: continuous mystery run
- R4: After the player solves a case, the next case starts automatically instead of the
  game ending.
- R5: Cases get harder as the run goes on: more suspects, more clues, and some misleading
  clues (red herrings) in later cases.
- R6: Cases vary in genre and severity (for example a missing heirloom, a poisoning, a
  locked-room murder, a cold case). At least 4 new original cases, for 7 or more in all.
- R7: The game shows a case counter and difficulty level (for example
  "Case 3 · Severity: Grave").
- R8: Failing a case (3 wrong guesses) ends the run and shows how many cases were solved.
